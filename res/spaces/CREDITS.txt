Cistern's scanned spaces are photogrammetry scans, converted to room geometry
by tools/cistern-convert.cpp (reduced to their air, smoothed, materials
assigned; the Pantheon scaled by 1.72 to its real 43.3 m and its oculus
re-opened, Leake Street scaled by 1.64 to its real ~300 m). All are licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

salt.bin        "Salt Tower Lower Room" (https://skfb.ly/oNoqN) by artfletch

The Dan Harpole Cistern is built from its published dimensions, not a scan.
